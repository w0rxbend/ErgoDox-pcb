## What changed on 2026-11-12

The earlier boards could not be built. These faults were fixed in the generator:

- Hotswap sockets were on the switch side. Sockets, diodes, LEDs and the controller are now on the back. USB-C and the buttons are on the front.
- The 4 alternate thumb sockets overlapped the main thumb sockets. They are removed.
- The USB-C receptacles faced into the board.
- The left board mirrored footprints, which reverses the pin order of every chip. It now mirrors positions only.
- The six transistors had no nets on the PCB. They are now 2N7002 (C8545) and MMBFJ177LT1G (C106763).
- The 5050 LEDs faced away from the keys. They are now reverse-mount SK6812MINI-E (C5149201).
- The home-made maze router is deleted. Routing goes through Freerouting 2.5.0.
- Six pulldown resistors on unused GPIO are removed. Firmware uses the internal pulldowns.
- The legacy TRRS notch in `Edge.Cuts` is filled so the LINK receptacle has board under its mouth.

## Next steps

1. Finish routing. Run `hw/ergodox-rp2040/tools/build.sh <freerouting.jar>` and read `reports/*-drc.json`. If connections stay open at the RP2040, give it more room (`U1_MOAT`, `GAP` in `tools/place.py`) or route the QSPI, crystal and USB nets in `place.py` before the router runs.
2. Reach 0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected items and 0 schematic mismatches on both boards.
3. Check the USB D+/D- pair. The router does not couple pairs. `DESIGN.md` section 13 asks for 0.2907 mm tracks with a 0.2032 mm gap, side by side.
4. Export Gerber, drill, BOM and CPL under `hw/ergodox-rp2040/fab/`, and write `ORDER.md`.
5. Build the four ZMK images.

## Open risks

- No level shifter on the LED data line. See `DESIGN.md` section 11.
- The 36 kΩ, 100 kΩ and 200 kΩ resistors have no verified LCSC number.
- The USB-C openings were not test fitted in the Litster case. USB-C is wider than the mini-USB and TRRS openings.
- JFET Rd disconnect: the MMBFJ177 Vgs(off) range was not re-read against a 3.3 V gate.
- SW0:12 on the right board is turned 180° to clear a mounting hole.

## Rules that still hold

- `ErgoDOX pcb/` and `ErgoDox Acrylic Case/` are read-only.
- Do not invent LCSC numbers. Leave the cell blank when no page was opened.
- Do not upload or pay for a fabrication order.
- Role stays compile-time: central image on the half plugged into the computer.
